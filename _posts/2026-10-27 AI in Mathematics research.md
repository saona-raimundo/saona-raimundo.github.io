This is a highlight of [First Proof](https://1stproof.org), who provides independent, transparent, and rigorous engagement with the evolving capabilities of AI in research mathematics. They offer clarity to the mathematical community and to the public so as to ground conversations about the role of AI tools.

For example, their [third benchmark](https://1stproof.org/third-batch.html) had the following conclusions in [its press-release](https://1stproof.org/third-batch-press-release.html):

- Open-weight systems are comparable to commercial models from December 2025.
- Writing and citation still lag, with a clear lag in exposition.
