I was the author of a blog featuring a professor!

I interviewed Ehud Shapiro is a scientist, entrepreneur, artist, and political activist. He is Professor Emeritus of Computer Science and Biology at the Weizmann Institute of Science and Visiting Professor at the Department of Mathematics and DSI at LSE. On 2 March 2026, he will give a public lecture titled “Grassroots: shaping the digital realm and through it – the world”.
